Our new project team member, Kajo Podrug, who recently joined the project as a PhD researcher at the Faculty of Civil Engineering, Architecture and Geodesy in Split, has achieved outstanding international recognition.

Kajo participated in the 2025 IRCK International Training Course on “Karst and Resources: Fragile Environment and Sustainable Development”, held in Guilin and Chongqing, China, and organized by the International Research Centre on Karst under the auspices of UNESCO. After completing the full two-week scientific and field programme, he received the Excellent Trainee Award – 2nd place, selected through a competitive evaluation among more than 30 international participants.
