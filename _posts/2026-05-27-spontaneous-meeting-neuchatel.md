As part of the From Sea to Summit project, a working meeting was held at the University of Neuchâtel (UNINE-CHYN).
The meeting brought together project leaders and researchers from both partner institutions. 
Discussions focused on the progress of joint research activities and publications, further development of the project database infrastructure, and integration of monitoring and hydrogeological datasets collected in Croatia and Switzerland. Particular attention was given to establishing efficient workflows for data management, sharing, and future data analysis that will support comparative studies between the Vis Island freshwater lens and the Tsanfleuron Karst System. 
The team also reviewed preparations for the upcoming Eurokarst Conference, where several project-related contributions will be presented.

From Sea to Summit is jointly funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) and the Croatian Science Foundation (HRZZ).
